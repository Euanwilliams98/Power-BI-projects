# Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

I built this dashboard to practise working with sales data in Power BI. It brings the main figures into one report and lets users compare sales and profit by product, category, region and customer segment.

## How I built the report

1. Imported the sales data into Power BI.
2. Used Power Query to check the columns, set the data types and prepare the data.
3. Created relationships between the tables.
4. Wrote DAX measures for the main sales and profit figures.
5. Added KPI cards, charts, maps and slicers.
6. Checked the report totals against the prepared data.

## What I found

The report can be filtered to compare different products, categories, regions and customer segments. Some of the results that stood out were:

- Technology had the highest sales at approximately £886K, followed by Office Supplies at £824K and Furniture at £639K.
- Consumer customers accounted for 52.64% of sales. Corporate contributed 31.43%, and Home Office 15.93%.
- Sales increased each year shown, from around £0.42M in 2011 to £0.75M in 2014.
- Profit also increased over this period, from approximately £57K to £85K.

## What I learned

This project gave me more practice with Power Query, data relationships and DAX. I also learned how to use cards, charts and filters to make a report that people can explore rather than just showing a table of figures.

## Tools and techniques

Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data modelling, data checks, KPI cards, charts, maps and interactive filters.
